What I do

Devices, virtual and real

I design and help build Virtual and Analog instruments. I specialise in Synth Mods mainly their Panels, but also circuit bending and mods. I also build plugin instruments and effects that run natively on any platform. I have a sweet spot for Erica Synths stuff & the Akai Force and MPC standalone units in a virtual environment. Most stuff on this repo are ports or re-imaginings of hardware I like to use, with the extras I wanted on stage: randomisers, presets, limiters, sequencers and patch bays you wire by touch.

  • 01
    Plugin instruments

    Drum machines and synths packaged as native VST2 plugins for MPC OS, each with its own ART skin.

  • 02
    Effects

    Remix effects, reverbs, glitch and slicer tools, tuners and radios recorded straight into the units.

  • 03
    Hardware and controllers

    Custom MIDI controllers and panel mod work. Circuit bends and add-ons. EURORACK modules and paraphernalia

  • 04
    Open source

    Everything is published on GitHub under L'Cronx, with releases ready to download & install or rebuild. Unless it is my own code.

Install

On the Force in three steps

Add the catalog

Open mpc-installer or mpc-catalog on your Force / MPC and point it at the L'Cronx releases. Optionally download the armv7.zip file and drag it into the mpc-catalog / installer.

Pick a plugin

Every release ships with install.sh and uninstall.sh, so a plugin goes on and comes off cleanly.

Load it

The plugin shows up as an instrument or insert in MPC OS with its own screen and Q-Link pages.

Wrapper and catalog tooling: github.com/sunskiefer/mpc-vst-plugins
